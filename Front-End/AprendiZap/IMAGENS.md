# Imagens ilustrativas

Método: recurso integrado de geração de imagens, uma chamada para cada imagem. Os arquivos foram convertidos para WebP, mantendo dimensões e composição. Os recortes usados nos cartões são feitos no CSS.

## hero.webp

Dimensões: 1536x1024.

Prompt utilizado:

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: wide website hero image for AprendiZap, a free Brazilian volunteer tutoring service.
Primary request: cheerful high-quality realistic editorial advertising photograph of exactly six diverse Brazilian young adult students aged 18–23 smiling together, holding colorful books and notebooks.
Scene/backdrop: saturated electric royal-blue and deep cobalt studio background, subtle small white hand-drawn education doodles around the group and abstract cyan ribbons.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, ideally 1536 × 1024. All six students occupy the RIGHT TWO THIRDS of the frame, some seated and some standing in a warm natural group. LEFT THIRD remains clean uninterrupted deep cobalt negative space suitable for website heading overlay. Frame comfortably and keep faces clear.
Lighting/mood: polished soft studio photography, lively and optimistic, realistic skin textures and natural candid expressions.
Color palette: vivid cobalt, royal blue, midnight navy, bright cyan accents, casual clothing predominantly blue and white.
Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, logos or watermark. Fictional illustrative students, not portraits of real volunteers. No extra people.
```

## estudante.webp

Dimensões: 1254x1254.

Prompt utilizado:

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: square website image for AprendiZap, a free Brazilian volunteer tutoring service.
Primary request: high-quality realistic editorial advertising photograph of a young Brazilian woman aged 20 with beautiful curly dark hair, wearing a blue top and holding a notebook while seated at a study desk. She smiles naturally, conveying focus and confidence.
Scene/backdrop: vivid deep cobalt blue studio environment, tasteful abstract cyan ribbon accents and small subtle white hand-drawn education doodles. A small stack of books at the desk.
Composition/framing: square 1:1, medium portrait at desk with clear face and notebook. Composition fills square gracefully.
Lighting/mood: polished soft studio photography, optimistic and welcoming, realistic skin and hair.
Color palette: vivid cobalt and royal blue with bright cyan accents, midnight navy shadows.
Constraints: no readable text, letters, numbers, logos or watermark. Fictional illustrative student.
```

## voluntario.webp

Dimensões: 1536x1024.

Prompt utilizado:

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: landscape website section image for AprendiZap, a free Brazilian volunteer tutoring service.
Primary request: high-quality realistic editorial advertising photograph of a friendly Brazilian adult male teacher about age 35 wearing glasses, smiling warmly and holding a notebook.
Scene/backdrop: saturated electric blue and deep cobalt studio background with tasteful small white education line doodles and abstract cyan ribbon accents around teacher.
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, ideally 1536 × 1024. Teacher positioned entirely on RIGHT HALF, approximately waist-up. LEFT HALF remains clean deep cobalt negative space for website copy. Keep face clear and a comfortable margin above head.
Lighting/mood: polished soft studio photography, approachable and helpful, realistic natural skin, casual blue clothing.
Color palette: royal blue and cobalt, midnight navy depth and bright cyan accents.
Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, logos or watermark. Fictional illustrative teacher, not an identity of a real volunteer. One person only.
```
